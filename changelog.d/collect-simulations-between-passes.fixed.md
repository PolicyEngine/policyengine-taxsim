PolicyEngineRunner now frees each pass's simulations before the next pass or chunk builds its own. After the first pass of a mixed state-0 chunk and after every chunk, including a failed one, it clears the reference that policyengine-us's cached pinned policies (and, with policyengine-us 1.x, the shared policy that `scorp_tax_benefit_system()` caches) keep to the finished simulation, then runs a full garbage collection.
