Output amounts are 64-bit floats rounded to the cent, and `taxsimid`, `year` and `state` are integers when their values are whole numbers. Inputs mixing years before 2021 with later ones printed `4009.199951171875` for 4009.20 and `1.0` for record 1. PolicyEngine amounts were also rounded in 32-bit arithmetic, which could leave one a cent off (most often above about $42,000); a CSV printed some amounts from $131,072 up with one decimal place, or with pandas 3 in scientific notation from $1 million; and a `.dta` file stored them as Stata floats.
